**Bars since 2026-09-28 (one night of user trials, all live by `9f6c72a6`):** every bar is COMPOSED on `<body>` in `tokens.css` — `--k-bar: var(--k-bar-lines), var(--k-bar-wash), var(--k-bar-shade), linear-gradient(var(--k-bar-tint), var(--k-bar-tint))`; pages only set `--k-bar-tint` (a `var()` inside a custom property resolves where it is DECLARED, so composing on `:root` would freeze `:root`'s tint — hence `body`). Layers: `--k-bar-lines` = a 1 px ink line every 3 px (print-screen texture; paper lines on the ink News bar), `--k-bar-wash` = 4 % paper (barely visible, kept at his number), `--k-bar-shade` = 38 % ink at both ends fading out by 34 %/66 %. Frames (logo ring, DE/EN pill, search/bell/avatar discs) are PAPER on every bar since that night (until then ink on the ochre bars, where the ink hairline had nothing to read against); the Profil active-avatar ring was dropped for the same reason. Tints: Markt `#d68a1a` with PAPER text (four tones tried live: `#b97a1a`, `#c88219`, `#d68a1a`, `#e89417`, then up to `#faaf2e` unseen and back down; paper on it is ~2.8:1 for the small labels, ink was 4.8 — he accepted), the search results page (`page="search"`) wears the same tint, the Profil bars are a fade of all six section colours in nav order with INK AT BOTH EDGES (ink in the middle swallowed the tab frames; hard full-width stripes cut through the wordmark and were rejected). ### Page inset rule (kiosk, 2026-09-19)
Every main section's page-level wrappers use ONE side inset: **16 px on phones · 36 px from `md` · 40 px from `lg`** — `px-4 md:px-9 lg:px-10` (margins: `mx-4 md:mx-9 lg:mx-10`; in plain CSS: 16 / `min-width: 768px` 36 / `min-width: 1024px` 40). Until that day each section set its own: forum 16/32/40, calendar 16/36/40, news 16/36/36, Markt 20/20/36 with its lead card at 12 (heading and cards did not share an edge), Kiez-Daten 20 (its station strip 18)/20/36 — the user saw it as „different indents". There is no shared wrapper component; when adding a page-level block, copy the three classes. Deliberate exceptions: the calendar month grid runs edge to edge on phones, the blog's centred masthead and 24 px column, the News nameplate („Schillerkiez Kurier", inset 36 px at every width), the Markt pager (centred). Measure with `scratchpad/indents.cjs` (prod, heading + first block, four widths) and `scratchpad/indent-edges.cjs` (every boxed block's left/right edge; env `PROBE_WIDTHS`). **Page width (decided 2026-09-20, „centre all three"):** every main section sits in ONE centred 1280 px column, like the masthead (`max-w-7xl`). Forum carries it on its `<main>`, Kiez-Daten on the orchestrator root, and calendar / News / Markt get it from a plain `<div class="mx-auto w-full max-w-[1280px]">` around the island in their `.astro` page (a plain block — no transform/filter — so fixed and sticky children keep working). Before that day those three had no maximum width: on a 1600 px screen their headings sat at 36–40 px while logo, forum and Kiez-Daten sat at ~200. Measured after: all five headings at 40 / 200 / 360 px on 1280 / 1600 / 1920 px screens, phones unchanged. A new main section must bring the same wrapper.
